Privacy Policy

Steply Chrome Extension Effective: May 29, 2026 Last updated: May 29, 2026
🔒

The short version: Steply stores everything locally on your device using IndexedDB. We collect no personal data, send nothing to any server, require no account, and run no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your recordings stay on your machine — always.

What Steply does

Steply is a Chrome extension that records your browser interactions — clicks, scrolls, and navigation — and turns them into step-by-step guides with screenshots. All of this happens entirely within your browser.

Data we collect

  • ✓ No personal data collected. We do not collect your name, email, IP address, or any identifying information.
  • ✓ No data sent to servers. Steply has no backend. Nothing you record is transmitted anywhere.
  • ✓ No account required. You install and use Steply without signing up or logging in.
  • ✓ No analytics or tracking. We use no third-party analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking tools.
  • ✓ Local storage only. All guides, steps, and screenshots are stored in your browser's IndexedDB — local to your device.
  • ✓ Local Redaction. The redaction tool (blurring) runs entirely on your local machine using an offscreen canvas. No image data is ever sent to a server for processing.

Permissions explained

Steply requests the following Chrome permissions. Here is exactly why each one is needed:

<all_urls>

Required to capture screenshots on any website the user explicitly chooses to record. Steply only activates on a page when you press Start Recording — it does not read, scan, or interact with any page passively.

activeTab

Required to access the currently active tab when you trigger a recording. Used solely to capture the screenshot of the page at the moment of each recorded step.

storage

Required to persist your recorded guides locally in IndexedDB between browser sessions. No data leaves your device.

Data storage & retention

All guides and screenshots created by Steply are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device. You can delete any guide at any time from the Steply dashboard, which permanently removes it from local storage.

Uninstalling the Steply extension from Chrome will remove all locally stored data.

Third-party services

Steply does not integrate with, share data with, or rely on any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, or external data processors involved.

Children's privacy

Steply is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, no special handling is required.
