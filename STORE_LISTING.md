# Steply - Your Instant Step-by-Step Guide Creator

**Steply** is the fastest, most beautiful way to capture your workflow and instantly turn it into a professional step-by-step guide. 

Stop wasting time taking manual screenshots and formatting documents. Simply click "Record", go about your task, and let Steply do the heavy lifting. Every click and scroll is automatically captured, highlighted, and assembled into a pristine guide.

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **Zero-Friction Capture:** Hit record and work naturally. Steply silently tracks your clicks and scrolls, capturing high-quality screenshots along the way.
*   **The Trinity Toggle:** Instantly switch visual frequency—choose **Flare (Red)**, **Orbit (Green)**, or **None**. Customize globally or per-step!
*   **Annotation Toolbar (New):** Draw attention exactly where you need it. Add arrows, boxes, text labels and auto-numbered badges in six colours, then drag them around or pull a corner to resize until they sit right. Your markup carries into every export and every copy-paste, and stays editable forever — the original screenshot is never overwritten.
*   **Edit Screenshot — with Smart Blur:** One workspace for all of it. Mark a screenshot up, and blur anything sensitive with a single drag: our Smart Blur algorithm ensures PII, passwords and keys are irreversibly obscured before you export or share.
*   **Ultimate Single-Click Copy (New):** Share faster than ever. Copy a step’s Title, Description, and Screenshot all at once. Paste into Slack, Gmail, or Teams—the entire step appears perfectly formatted with the image!
*   **Professional Dashboard:** A sleek, high-performance UI to manage your library. Edit action text, add notes, and organize steps effortlessly.
*   **Bulk Export Manager:** Combine multiple guides into one professional document—perfect for complex onboarding or full-system documentation.
*   **Save Any Page as PDF (New):** One click scrolls the entire page—past the fold, all the way down—and saves it as a clean, multi-page A4 PDF. It works on web apps that scroll an inner panel, waits for lazy-loaded images so they don't come out blank, and strips pinned headers and cookie banners so they don't repeat on every page. If a page can't be captured in full, the PDF says so instead of pretending. Nothing is saved as a guide; you just get the file.
*   **Privacy & Security First:** Steply is 100% local with absolutely no remotely hosted code. No servers, no telemetry. Your guides stay on your machine.

## 🛠 How it Works

1.  Open the Steply popup — click the icon or press **Alt+Shift+G** — and click **Start Recording**.
2.  Navigate any website. Every time you click or scroll, Steply captures the screen and logs the action.
3.  Click **Stop Recording** when you're done.
4.  Open the **Dashboard** to review your new guide, tweak the highlight colors, mark up any screenshot with arrows and labels, and export it as a PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML or JSON file!

Need the page itself rather than a walkthrough of it? Open the popup and click **Save current page as PDF** — no recording required.

Whether you're creating onboarding materials, bug reports, or standard operating procedures (SOPs), Steply makes documentation effortless and elegant.
